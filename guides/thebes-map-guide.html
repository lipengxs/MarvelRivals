First three matches: a useful Thebes review

  1. Match one — find the objective problem: identify the space the team must reach or preserve before discussing hero changes.
  2. Match two — test one entry: agree on one route, the player who begins pressure and the teammate who follows that timing.
  3. Match three — test the reset: name the point where a lost attempt ends and the group can safely regroup. Do not turn a late retreat into staggered fights.

Record one concrete result after each attempt: the entry failed, protection broke, focus was unclear, objective presence was lost, or reset was late. Then change only the matching team job.
