INDEPENDENT EDITORIAL NOTES · REVIEWED JULY 29, 2026

Marvel Rivals editorial notes

These notes explain how to turn a source into a useful player decision without turning an announcement, one video or one external statistic into a false permanent Meta claim.

Editorial rule: official facts, editorial frameworks and player observations are labelled differently. If a version, scope or original source cannot be verified, the conclusion stays Unconfirmed.

UPDATE READING · JULY 29, 2026

Read a hero or patch update in the right order

  1. Open the original official post and record its URL, original date, named version and applicable scope.
  2. Separate what the post explicitly confirms from what it does not say about win rates, counters or rankings.
  3. Check affected hero, Team-Up and map pages for historical details that need re-verification.
  4. Turn the update into one testable player question, such as whether a team needs a clearer entry or reset—not an instant universal Tier List.

See the decision-data policy and the 20260723 source review for the applied format.

MAP NOTE · JULY 29, 2026

Thebes: record decisions before declaring a map solved

The official 20260723 patch record confirms Thebes as new content. It does not establish best heroes, optimal routes or rank-specific win rates. A useful first-week map note therefore records the objective problem, entry attempt, protection failure or reset timing that the group actually observed.

Use the Thebes first-match guide to create that record, then update a map-specific conclusion only when the source and method are visible.

EXTERNAL DATA NOTE

What a tracker number can—and cannot—tell you

A third-party number is context only until its patch, mode, platform, rank range, sample size and collection period are visible. It can help formulate a review question; it cannot prove that a hero is best for every map or player. Read the Tracker guidance before acting on an external statistic.
