How this tool works

Roles come from the source-linked hero registry. The job labels are an editorial framework: Duelists contribute pressure and finishing, Vanguards contribute space and objective presence, and Strategists contribute protection and reset support. A real composition still depends on player comfort, communication, the current version and confirmed Team-Up information.
