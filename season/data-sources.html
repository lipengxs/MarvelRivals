Marvel Rivals decision data and source policy

A useful decision tool should explain both its recommendation and its uncertainty. This guide separates documented game information from editorial planning advice, player-supplied match context and third-party observations. It does not access player accounts, game clients, private APIs or hidden match data.

What a decision needs

Before acting on a hero or lineup recommendation, check the current version, role coverage, mode and objective, entry and regroup route, player comfort, communication plan, and any dated official Team-Up confirmation. A single popular pick, clip or tracker number is not enough on its own.

Where information comes from

  1. Official Marvel Rivals pages and formal announcements are the publication source for roster, role, patch, rule and dated Team-Up claims. The official page is linked rather than copied without context.
  2. Official video or social announcements may flag a change for review, but a decision page remains marked Unconfirmed until its exact dated source and scope are recorded.
  3. Public third-party tools and event pages are optional context, not a replacement for official patch information. Their methodology must travel with any cited observation.
  4. GSC and site analytics identify what visitors are trying to learn. They guide content priorities only; they do not determine hero strength or player outcomes.

Freshness and correction rules

  • When an official update appears, affected hero cards, Team-Up references and composition wording are reviewed before a patch-specific conclusion is published.
  • If a version, source date or scope cannot be verified, the page says Unconfirmed rather than filling the gap with a tier list or derived statistic.
  • Third-party figures expire when their collection period or applicable version is unavailable.
  • Editorial checklists are reviewed at least every 90 days and again when a documented rule change affects them.

How to use this site

Start with the Season Hub, then choose a hero profile for source-linked role context or open Team Composer to test coverage and produce a pre-match checklist. For a third-party statistic, read the separate Tracker guidance before treating it as evidence.
