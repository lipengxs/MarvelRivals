Marvel Rivals Version 20260723 patch notes: what is confirmed

This is an independent reading guide for the official Version 20260723 patch notes. The official post says the patch was scheduled for July 23, 2026 at 09:00 UTC with no server downtime. It is not a hero tier list or a claim about live win rates.

Primary source: Marvel Rivals Version 20260723 Patch Notes. Every factual item below is limited to that dated official post.

Confirmed in the official post

New map: Thebes

The post introduces Thebes as new content. Its description references Apocalypse's Pyramid and the Temple of Khonshu. This confirms the map announcement; it does not establish a universal best hero or route. Read the Thebes first-match guide.

Esports subscriptions

The Esports tab receives a subscription feature, calendar view, match schedules, standings, brackets, historical records and notifications for subscribed matches, according to the official post.

Team-Up VFX preference

A settings option lets a player enable or disable Team-Up visual effects on their own screen, globally or per hero. The source says teammates and enemies still see the effects. This is a visual preference—not evidence that a particular Team-Up pairing is active.

Twitch Drops period

The official post lists a Mid-Season Finals Twitch Drops period from July 29, 2026 at 17:00 UTC through August 29, 2026 at 17:00 UTC. Confirm eligible channels and current requirements through official channels before watching.

Documented fixes

The source documents a spectator UI fix, an ammo-count display fix for The Punisher's Culling Turret, and costume weapon-asset fixes for Angela, Daredevil and Gambit. These are documented fixes, not balance-value changes or a reason to label any hero stronger or weaker.
